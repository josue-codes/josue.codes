## My Goal

I want everyone to be able to use this site, however they browse. The target is the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), level AA. This statement is voluntary.

## What Is in Place

- Semantic HTML, with headings and landmarks that describe the page.
- A skip link to the main content.
- Keyboard navigation with a visible focus outline.
- Text and link color contrast that meets AA in all four color themes, in both light and dark mode.
- Theme and light/dark controls, so you can pick the combination that is easiest for you to read.
- Reduced-motion settings are respected.
- The site works without JavaScript. The default theme applies and all content stays readable.
- Glossary terms in case studies open by tap, keyboard or hover, and every term is also listed in full at the end of each case study.
- Images have text alternatives, or are marked as decorative.

## Limits

The site has not had an independent accessibility audit. I check color contrast against the WCAG formula and review pages in desktop and mobile browsers myself. Those checks can miss things.
